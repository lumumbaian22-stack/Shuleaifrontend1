Shule AI Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: June 4, 2026  •  Applies where a school uses Shule AI to process school-controlled personal data.

Plain meaning: The school controls most school records. Shule AI processes those records to operate the platform, protect access, provide support, and deliver plan-based services.

1. Controller and processor roles

For school-owned records, the school is normally the data controller because it decides why the records are collected, who should access them, and how they are used for school administration, learning, reporting, fees, communications, and safeguarding.

Shule AI is normally the data processor for those school-owned records because it processes them to provide the platform and related services. Shule AI may act as an independent controller for limited platform administration data such as billing, security logs, fraud prevention, platform analytics, support records, and legal compliance records.

2. Subject matter, duration, and categories of processing

Processing continues for the term of the school’s use of Shule AI and for any retention period needed for backups, audit, legal, billing, security, or dispute purposes.

3. School instructions and responsibilities

Shule AI will process school-controlled personal data according to the school’s platform configuration, user actions, subscription settings, written instructions, and this DPA, unless legally required otherwise.

The school is responsible for:

  • having a lawful basis and required authority/consent for data uploaded or created in Shule AI;
  • approving correct users and removing users who should no longer have access;
  • maintaining accurate parent-child links, student records, fee records, marks, comments, signatures, communications, and subject assignments;
  • ensuring announcements, SMS, alerts, and AI prompts are lawful, accurate, and appropriate;
  • responding to school-level data-subject requests unless Shule AI support is needed.

4. Security measures

Shule AI will use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate for the platform, including authentication, role permissions, school-code scoping, parent-child ownership checks, backend feature gates, audit logs, access restrictions, backup practices, and provider safeguards.

The school must maintain security on its side, including strong user management, responsible device use, credential confidentiality, timely removal of departed users, and review of role assignments.

5. Subprocessors and integrations

Shule AI may use subprocessors and integrations for hosting, storage, SMS, payments, email, AI, analytics, error monitoring, and support. Shule AI will use providers only as needed to provide the service and will take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate data-protection safeguards.

The school authorizes Shule AI to use such subprocessors as part of the platform. Where required, Shule AI may update the list of subprocessors or provider categories through the platform or support documentation.

6. Assistance with rights, corrections, and audits

Shule AI will provide reasonable assistance, where technically possible, for correction, export, deletion, unlinking, parent-child audit cleanup, security review, data-subject requests, and compliance support. Some actions may require school admin approval or Super Admin verification.

7. Personal-data incidents

Shule AI will investigate suspected unauthorized access or disclosure affecting school-controlled data and notify the school where required by applicable law or where the school needs to take action. The school must promptly notify Shule AI of suspected incidents caused by school users, credentials, devices, exports, or configurations.

8. Return, deletion, and retention

At the end of service, the school may request export, return, deletion, or restriction of school-controlled records where legally and technically possible. Shule AI may retain limited data for legal, billing, audit, backup, dispute, fraud-prevention, and security reasons.

9. Liability, school indemnity, and legal limits

Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions. To the fullest extent allowed by law, the school will indemnify Shule AI against claims, penalties, losses, costs, or expenses arising from the school’s unlawful instructions, unauthorized data uploads, inaccurate records, wrong parent-child links, unlawful communications, lack of required consent/authority, or misuse by school-authorized users.

Shule AI remains responsible for its own fraud, wilful misconduct, and liability that cannot legally be excluded or shifted. Nothing in this DPA removes rights or obligations that cannot be removed under applicable law.

10. Conflict

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service, this DPA controls only for personal-data processing obligations. The Terms continue to control platform use, subscriptions, acceptable use, payments, and general liability.

This DPA is a platform template and should be reviewed by a qualified Kenyan advocate/data-protection professional before final production launch.
