Shule AI Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement describes how Shule AI processes school-controlled data on behalf of each school administrator.

Controller and processor

The school acts as the controller of school records. Shule AI acts as a processor for platform operation, support, security, and authorized service delivery.

Processing scope

Processing includes student records, parent links, teacher assignments, attendance, grades, fees, payments, communication, subscriptions, reports, and enabled AI usage.

Access controls

Access is limited by role, school code, subscription feature gates, and parent-child ownership rules.

Contact

DPA questions should be directed to the school administrator or Shule AI support.
