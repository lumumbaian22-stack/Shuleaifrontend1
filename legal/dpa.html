Shule AI Data Processing Agreement

Effective: June 4, 2026

This DPA describes how Shule AI processes school-controlled data on behalf of schools.

1. Controller and processor

The school controls school records. Shule AI processes those records to provide the platform.

2. Processing scope

Processing includes authentication, role access, student records, teacher records, parent links, assessments, report cards, payments, alerts, messages, SMS, timetable, calendar, duty, and analytics.

3. Confidentiality and access

Access is role-based and school-scoped. Super Admin access is limited to platform administration, approvals, billing, audit, and support.

4. Subprocessors and integrations

SMS, payment, hosting, and AI providers may process limited data needed to provide their services.

5. Retention and correction

Schools may request correction, unlinking, or audit cleanup for inaccurate records such as wrong parent-child links.
