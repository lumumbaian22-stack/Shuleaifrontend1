Shule AI Privacy Policy

Effective: June 4, 2026

Shule AI handles school operations data including users, students, classes, attendance, grades, fee records, subscriptions, messages, alerts, and school settings.

1. Data we process

We process account details, school records, communication records, academic records, billing status, and system audit data needed to run the platform.

2. School isolation

School-owned data is scoped to the authenticated user’s school code. Parent access is limited to verified linked children only.

3. Use of data

Data is used to provide dashboards, reports, alerts, analytics, subscriptions, SMS, tutoring access, and support.

4. AI features

AI is used for tutoring and message suggestions according to plan access. AI suggestions require human review before sending.

5. Security

Backend access checks protect features, roles, school identity, and parent-child ownership. Frontend hiding is not treated as security.
