Shule AI Privacy Policy

Shule AI processes school, teacher, parent, and student information to provide school operations, communication, analytics, billing, reporting, and learning-support features.

Data isolation

School-owned data is scoped by school code and role permissions. Parents access only linked children; students access only their own data.

Data use

Data is used to operate dashboards, attendance, homework, report cards, alerts, payments, timetable, calendar, messaging, subscriptions, and AI-assisted features where enabled.

Security

The platform applies backend access checks for protected modules and ownership checks for parent-child records.

Contact

Privacy questions should be directed to the school administrator or Shule AI support.
