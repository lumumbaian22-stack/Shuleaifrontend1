Plain meaning: Shule AI processes school and student information to run dashboards, communications, report cards, subscriptions, analytics, SMS, AI tutor access, and school operations. We use role-based and school-scoped access controls, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Who this Privacy Policy applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how Shule AI collects, receives, stores, uses, protects, and shares personal data in connection with the Shule AI school platform. It covers school administrators, teachers, parents/guardians, students, support users, platform administrators, and visitors to Shule AI pages.

For school-controlled records, the school normally determines why and how data is entered and used. Shule AI processes that data to provide the platform, support, security, billing, analytics, AI features, SMS, and related services.

3. Children’s data

Shule AI is designed for schools and student-success workflows. Children’s data may be processed where a school, parent, guardian, or authorized user provides it for lawful school administration, learning support, reporting, attendance, communication, or subscription access.

Schools are responsible for ensuring they have the required authority, parent/guardian consent, policy basis, or legal basis to enter and use child data. Shule AI applies school-scoped access and parent-child ownership checks so parents/guardians access only verified linked children.

4. How we use data

  • create and secure user accounts;
  • display the correct school, role, child, class, subject, and plan-based dashboard;
  • generate classes, subjects, report cards, analytics, attendance, timetable, calendar, homework, duty, and fee views;
  • send or display announcements, alerts, SMS, messages, and school updates;
  • manage school plans, parent-child subscriptions, trials, pilots, demo access, and manual overrides;
  • provide AI tutor and AI announcement suggestions where allowed;
  • detect misuse, repair wrong parent-child links, support audits, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability;
  • comply with legal, security, billing, dispute, and data-protection obligations.

5. Lawful basis and school responsibility

Depending on the context, processing may be based on contract, legitimate interests, consent, legal obligation, school/public-interest duties, safeguarding, or another lawful basis recognized by applicable law. Schools are responsible for giving appropriate notices and obtaining required approvals or consent for data they control and upload.

Users must not upload data they are not authorized to process. Schools must keep their data accurate, remove users who leave, correct wrong parent-child links, and ensure messages are sent only to proper audiences.

6. Sharing, subprocessors, and integrations

We may share limited data with service providers only where needed to operate Shule AI, including hosting, database, authentication, SMS, payment, email, analytics, AI, support, monitoring, and security providers. These providers may process data only for the service purpose and subject to applicable safeguards.

We may disclose data if required by law, valid legal process, security investigation, fraud prevention, child-safety concern, school authorization, payment dispute, or to protect rights, safety, and platform integrity.

We do not sell personal data.

7. AI features and human review

AI features may process prompts, student learning context, message purposes, tone, key points, and limited relevant records to provide tutoring or draft suggestions. AI outputs are not automatically sent. School users must review and approve generated announcements, alerts, and SMS before sending.

Where AI provider processing is used, only the information needed for the specific AI task should be sent. Schools should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive data into AI prompts.

8. Security and access controls

Shule AI uses authentication, role checks, school-code scoping, parent-child ownership checks, feature gates, audit logs, and backend access controls. Frontend hiding is not treated as security by itself.

No system is perfectly secure. Users must keep credentials private, use secure devices, avoid account sharing, and report suspected unauthorized access immediately.

9. Retention and deletion

School records are retained for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal/accounting/audit obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, support backups, or as instructed by the school. Some records may remain in backups or logs for a limited period after deletion.

Schools may request correction, export, unlinking, deletion, or audit cleanup where legally and technically possible, especially for wrong parent-child links or inaccurate records.

10. Data rights and requests

Depending on applicable law and context, users may have rights to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or complaint. Some requests must be handled through the school because the school controls the underlying education record.

Shule AI may verify identity, role, school authority, and parent-child link before responding to a request. We may refuse or limit a request where required or permitted by law, safety, audit, school obligations, or protection of another person’s rights.

11. Data incident and breach handling

If Shule AI becomes aware of a personal-data incident affecting school-controlled data, we will investigate, take reasonable containment steps, and notify the affected school or relevant authority where required by applicable law. Schools must notify Shule AI promptly if they suspect unauthorized access through their users, devices, credentials, exports, or school workflows.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, law, providers, AI features, payment flows, or school workflows change. Material updates may be shown inside the platform and may require fresh acceptance.

This Privacy Policy is a platform template and should be reviewed by a qualified Kenyan advocate/data-protection professional before final production launch.
